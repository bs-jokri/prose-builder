Title: Sunset Memory

It was a quiet evening in early September when Emma stood at the edge of the lake.
The water reflected the fiery orange and gold of the sky, and the wind whispered gently
through the trees. This was her favorite place — a hidden memory from childhood, now revived
as a moment of peace.

She closed her eyes, took a deep breath, and let the warmth of the sunset fill her chest.
Somehow, everything felt possible again.
